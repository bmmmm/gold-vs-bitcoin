🥇 Gold runs out first — by about a century.

Known gold reserves are exhausted around 2044. The last satoshi won't be mined until ~2140.

* "Runs out" means: known economic reserves ÷ current mine production. That number has hovered near two decades' worth for, well, decades — new deposits keep getting found and reclassified. Bitcoin's finish line, by contrast, is fixed in the protocol.

* Bitcoin is already ~95% mined, yet the last 5% takes another century — the block reward halves every 210,000 blocks, so issuance approaches zero asymptotically.
